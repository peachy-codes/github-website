## Overview

**Street Spirit** is a grassroots, independent newspaper produced and sold by unhoused and low-income vendors in the San Francisco East Bay, covering housing justice, community survival, and local governance.

---

## Mentee Project: Swept Off The Map

- **[Swept Off The Map](https://sweptoffthemap.com):** A community data journalism and mapping project produced through direct data mentorship with Street Spirit. 
- The project documents, visualizes, and maps municipal encampment sweeps across the Bay Area, providing interactive spatial evidence of displacement patterns, property confiscation, and public policy impacts.

---

## Contributions & Mentorship

- **Data Mentorship & Project Guidance:** Mentored grassroots advocates and journalists in data collection methodologies, spatial mapping, cleaning public records, and quantitative verification for investigative stories.
- **Municipal & Housing Data Analysis:** Analyzed public records requests (PRA), municipal sweep logs, and shelter availability metrics to support evidence-based reporting.
- **Accessible Tooling:** Developed Python and spreadsheet automation workflows to clean, geocode, and structure messy municipal datasets for community reporting.
