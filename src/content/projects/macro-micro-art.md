## Conceptual Framework

**MACRO/micro** is an interpretative, multi-layered visual work exploring the unbalanced feedback loops between human creative culture and artificial intelligence systems. 

Through generative multi-scale textures and visual decay, the piece examines three core thematic phases:
1. **Consumption:** The systematic extraction and ingesting of collective human expression.
2. **Adoption:** The pervasive normalization and integration of synthetic systems into daily cognition and creative labor.
3. **Assimilation:** The flattening and re-absorption of cultural forms into algorithmic feedback loops.
